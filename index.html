Answers

Frequently asked questions

Everything people ask before their first rental.

What is TRON energy?

Energy is the resource the TRON network spends when it runs a smart contract, including every USDT (TRC-20) transfer. If your wallet holds enough energy, the transfer costs no TRX. If it doesn't, TRON burns your TRX instead — which is why a simple USDT send can cost several TRX.

How much energy does a USDT transfer need?

About 65,000 energy when the receiving wallet already holds USDT, and about 131,000 for the first transfer to a wallet that has never held USDT before. If you're unsure, choose 131,000 — extra energy is simply unused.

How do I fix the OUT_OF_ENERGY error on TRON?

OUT_OF_ENERGY means the sending wallet ran out of energy mid-transaction. Rent energy to that same sending address, then retry the transfer. Delivery takes under 10 seconds and the transaction goes through normally.

Do I need to connect my wallet or share my private key?

No. Energy is delegated to your public TRON address — the address anyone can already see on the blockchain. You never connect a wallet, sign a message, or share a private key or seed phrase. A public address on its own cannot be used to move your funds.

Is renting energy cheaper than staking TRX?

For most users, yes. Staking means locking a large amount of TRX for 14 days to generate energy, and that capital stays frozen the whole time. Renting costs a small fee per transfer with nothing locked up, which works out cheaper unless you're sending USDT constantly at high volume.

How long does rented energy last?

One hour from delivery. Use it for as many transfers as the energy covers within that window. Anything left over returns to the network when the hour ends.

How fast is delivery?

Under 10 seconds in normal conditions. You get the delegation transaction hash as soon as it confirms, so you can verify delivery yourself on TronScan.

What happens if an order fails?

Your balance is refunded automatically. You're only charged for energy that actually reaches your address — which is how the success rate stays at 99.99%.

Which TRON wallets work with this?

All of them. Energy is delegated to a TRON address, so TronLink, Trust Wallet, TokenPocket, OKX Wallet, Ledger, SafePal, Binance Web3 Wallet or any other wallet holding a TRON address works the same way. Just paste the address.

Can I rent energy for someone else's address?

Yes. Energy goes to whatever TRON address you enter, so you can send it to a colleague, a client, or one of your own cold wallets. The receiving address doesn't need to do anything.

Why did my transfer still fail after renting energy?

Usually one of three reasons: the energy went to the receiving address instead of the sending address, the transfer needed 131,000 energy because the receiver had never held USDT, or the hour expired before you sent. Energy must always go to the wallet that signs the transaction.

Do you offer a TRON energy API?

Not yet — it is in development. Exchanges, payment processors and wallet teams will be able to automate energy purchases instead of ordering manually. Message support on Telegram for access.
