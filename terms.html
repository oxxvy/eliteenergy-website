Definitions

Service
The EliteEnergy Telegram bot, this website, and the energy rental function they provide.
Energy
A resource of the TRON network consumed when a smart contract executes, including TRC-20 transfers such as USDT.
Delegation
The on-chain action by which energy is made available to a TRON address for a period of time.
Order
A request you submit through the bot for a stated amount of energy to a stated address.
Balance
The TRX credited to you inside the bot after a deposit confirms, used to pay for orders.
Recipient address
The public TRON address you nominate to receive delegated energy.
TRON network
The public TRON blockchain, operated by its validators and governance — not by us.

What EliteEnergy does — and who does what

It matters that you understand where our responsibility begins and ends. Five separate parties are involved in a rental, and we are only one of them.

  • EliteEnergy accepts your order, charges your balance, and submits a delegation transaction to the TRON network.
  • The TRON network validates and settles that transaction, and defines what energy is, what it costs, and how much a transfer requires. We neither operate nor control it, and we cannot alter or reverse anything it has recorded.
  • Telegram provides the messaging platform the bot runs on. It is an independent company with its own terms and its own availability.
  • Your wallet holds your keys and signs your transactions. We have no access to it and cannot move your funds.
  • Third-party exchanges, wallets and explorers you use alongside the service are independent of us.

EliteEnergy is not a wallet, an exchange, a broker, a custodian, or a financial institution. We do not hold your USDT or other tokens, and we do not provide financial, investment, tax or legal advice.

06

Energy rental and delegation

When you place an order, we delegate energy to the recipient address you specify, for the duration stated at purchase. Delegation is a standard TRON network operation: it grants the use of a resource to an address, and it does not give us — or anyone else — the ability to move assets held by that address.

Energy is delegated to the address you enter. It is not transferable to another address afterwards, and it cannot be converted back into TRX.

07

TRX deposits and your balance

Orders are paid from a TRX balance held inside the bot. You fund it by sending TRX to the deposit address the bot gives you. Your balance is credited once the network confirms the deposit.

Deposits are irreversible. Sending the wrong asset, using the wrong network, or sending to an address other than the one the bot issued to you may result in permanent loss that we cannot recover on your behalf.

08

Pricing

The price of each rental is shown in the bot before you confirm. Prices are quoted in TRX and may change at any time, including in response to conditions on the TRON network. The price that applies to an order is the one displayed at the moment you confirm it.

Prices published on this website are indicative. The bot is the authoritative source.

09

Placing an order

To place an order you select an amount of energy and provide a recipient address. You are responsible for both. Confirming an order authorises us to charge your balance and to submit the corresponding delegation.

How much energy a transfer needs is determined by the TRON network and by the state of the receiving wallet — not by us. Requirements can change. If you order less energy than your transaction consumes, the shortfall is charged by the network to the sending wallet in TRX.

10

Order processing and delivery

Orders are normally processed within seconds of confirmation. We do not guarantee any specific delivery time. Delivery depends on the TRON network accepting and confirming the transaction, which is outside our control and can be affected by congestion, resource limits, and network upgrades.

A completed order returns a transaction identifier. That identifier, and the record on the TRON blockchain, is the definitive evidence of what was delivered and when.

11

Rental duration and expiry

Energy is rented for the period stated at purchase and expires automatically at the end of it. Unused energy is not refundable, cannot be extended or carried over, and is not exchangeable for TRX or any other asset.

It is your responsibility to make your transaction within the rental period.

12

Failed orders and refunds

If an order is charged but energy is not delivered to the recipient address, the amount charged is returned to your balance.

Refunds are not available where the service performed as ordered. That includes, in particular:

  • Energy delivered to an address you entered incorrectly.
  • Energy delivered to a receiving wallet when the sending wallet needed it.
  • A rental that expired before you made your transaction.
  • An order for less energy than your transaction required.
  • A transaction that failed for reasons unrelated to energy, such as an insufficient token balance or a wallet or network error.

If you believe an order failed and has not been refunded, contact support with the order details.

13

Cancellation

An order cannot be cancelled once it has been submitted to the TRON network, because the underlying transaction cannot be recalled. Where an order has been confirmed but not yet submitted, contact support immediately; we will cancel it if it is still possible to do so, but we cannot promise that it will be.

  • The amount. Confirm what your transaction actually requires before ordering.
  • Your deposits. Correct asset, correct network, correct address.
  • Your Telegram account security, and verifying that you are talking to the official bot.
  • Your own legal and tax position in your jurisdiction.
